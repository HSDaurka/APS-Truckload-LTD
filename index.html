How shipping with APS works

Four simple steps from first call to signed delivery.

  1. Request a quote

    Send the pickup, delivery and freight details using the form or by phone.

  2. Confirm the load

    We reply with a rate and timing, then send a written load confirmation.

  3. Pickup and transit

    Your driver collects the freight on time and dispatch keeps you updated.

  4. Delivery and invoice

    We deliver, send the proof of delivery and follow with a clear invoice.
